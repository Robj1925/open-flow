Fair questions

What is the best dictation app for Mac?

It depends on privacy and budget. If you want free, private, on-device dictation on an Apple Silicon Mac, OpenFlow is the pick. If you are happy with a cloud subscription and want maximum polish, Wispr Flow is excellent. If you prefer to pay once, SuperWhisper covers live dictation and MacWhisper covers transcribing recordings.

What is the best free dictation app?

Apple Dictation, built into every Mac, is the zero-effort answer. If you want system-wide hold-a-key dictation with more accuracy and a custom dictionary, OpenFlow is also completely free, open source, and runs entirely on your Mac.

Which dictation apps work fully offline?

OpenFlow, SuperWhisper, and MacWhisper run entirely on your Mac. Apple Dictation processes on device on Apple Silicon Macs. On Windows, Voice Access is built in and works offline. Cloud services like Wispr Flow and Otter.ai need an internet connection.

Is Dragon still available for Mac?

No. Dragon for Mac was discontinued in 2018, and Nuance now ships Dragon for Windows only. Mac users need alternatives, which is exactly what this picker covers: OpenFlow for free local dictation, Wispr Flow for cloud polish, SuperWhisper or MacWhisper for one-time purchases.

Whatever you pick, try the free one first

OpenFlow gives you hold-a-key dictation in any app on your Mac, transcribed 100% on device. Free, open source, no account. If it is not for you, the paid apps will still be there tomorrow.
