Fair questions

How many words per minute is normal speech?

Conversational English runs about 140 to 160 words per minute. Presentations and prepared talks land closer to 110 to 130 wpm, because pauses, emphasis, and nerves all slow you down. Audiobook narrators aim for roughly 150 to 155 wpm.

How many words is a 5 minute speech?

About 700 words at a comfortable conversational pace of 140 wpm. A slow, deliberate delivery is closer to 550 words, and a fast talker can fit around 850. When in doubt, write 700 and rehearse with a timer.

Is speaking faster than typing?

Yes, by a wide margin. Most people speak around 140 wpm but type around 40 wpm, so speech is roughly 3x faster at getting words out of your head. That gap is exactly why dictation feels like a superpower once it clicks.

Can I write documents at speaking speed?

Yes, with dictation software. OpenFlow is a free, open source dictation app for macOS that transcribes 100% on device at your natural speaking pace: hold a key, talk, release, and the words appear at your cursor. No subscription, no cloud, no word limits.
