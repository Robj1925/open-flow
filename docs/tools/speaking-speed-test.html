Fair questions

What is the average speaking speed?

Most people speak conversational English at about 140 to 160 words per minute. Presenters slow down, sports commentators speed up, and audiobook narrators hold a steady 150 to 160 wpm so listeners never have to rewind.

What speed should I present at?

Aim for about 110 to 130 words per minute with deliberate pauses. It feels slow when you are the one speaking, but it gives an audience time to absorb each point, and the pauses read as confidence rather than hesitation.

Is speaking really faster than typing?

Yes, by a wide margin. The average typing speed is about 40 words per minute, so most people speak 3 to 4 times faster than they type. That gap is exactly why dictation works: you get thoughts out at talking speed instead of typing speed.

How can I change my pace?

Practice with a timer, pause at punctuation instead of rushing through it, and record yourself. Hearing your own recording is the fastest way to notice pacing habits, and rerunning this test gives you a number to track over time.
